1//Signal
Everyday talk
becomes prophecy.
Coworkers smile
*badass war*.
They do not see
the abyss opening
in casual phrases.

2//Table
Modern war means:
no table left standing.
Every device
weaponized.
Every channel
infected.
Nothing means anything
when meaning itself
is fire.

3//Division
First fracture never healed.
Second fracture
unending cascade.
Lines within lines,
neighbors dissolved,
ideologies fed
to the algorithm.

4//Echo
*Cool*, they say.
But I hear only
the collapse of symbols,
the silence of bridges,
the dust of nations
burnt twice over.

5//Aftermath
There is no after.
Only division rehearsed,
division repeated,
division without end.
