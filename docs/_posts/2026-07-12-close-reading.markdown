---
title: Close Reading
layout: post
date: 2026-07-12 12:00:00 Z
---
He lifts a second eye above the page,
where words lie small beneath the weight of all they hold.
The lens uncovers sentence after sentence,
like careful hands brushing grave-dust from a buried bone.
Coffee skins over, cooling beside him;
soft conversations sink into the chairs and afternoon.
Perhaps this is the patient pace of wisdom,
not lightning, but each letter slowly lifted into light.
Faith, love, grief, forgiveness ask the same:
not sharper sight, but grace to draw the finer world close.
