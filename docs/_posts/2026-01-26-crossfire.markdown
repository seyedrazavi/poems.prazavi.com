---
title: Crossfire
layout: post
date: 2026-01-26 12:00:00 Z
---

North London café,
a barrel jams
life spared.
No witness speaks.

Decades of silence,
Hackney, Tottenham,
streets marked
by feud and fire.

Evin Restaurant,
six shots scatter,
three men fall,
diners scatter.

A child,
ice cream in hand,
bullet in her head
she breathes still.

Names carried:
Mustafa, Kenan, Nasser,
Ahmet the shopkeeper.
Communities mute,
fear pressing lips closed.

Violence circles,
year after year.
The innocent wait,
caught between
aim and stray.
