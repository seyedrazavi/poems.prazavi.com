---
title: Beneath the Bridge
layout: post
date: 2026-08-01 10:00:00 Z
---
The bridge keeps still above the cut,
yet every footstep carries it elsewhere.
Water cradles two unsettled skies,
one cold with daylight, one darkened by memory.
A runner enters the tunnel’s mouth,
then breaks from shadow owing nothing to night.
Moored boats rehearse their perfect stillness,
while trees bow twice, to wind and wavering glass.
Each crossing asks which world you leave,
the canal bears clouds through darkness, never sky.
