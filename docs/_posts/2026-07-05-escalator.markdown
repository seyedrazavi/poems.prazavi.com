title: Escalator
layout: post
date: 2026-07-05 12:00:00 Z
---
We inherit gradients of weather,
black cloth gathers rain around one woman’s face.
Grey turns inward, a shuttered country,
crossed alone beneath the lift’s fluorescent hum.
Two mouths meet between the storeys,
while cranes hoist unfinished promises through cloud.
Far below, someone waits unseen,
held behind the glass like breath that will not clear.
The city carries us without judgement,
until one still lens binds our passing lives.
