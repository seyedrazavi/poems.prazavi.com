title: Coverciano
layout: post
date: 2025-11-12 12:00:00 Z
---
She is eighty-seven,
her arm gone stiff,
the kitchen too far.

A call at eight
fatigue in her voice,
the small word: hungry.

The door opens.
Two uniforms enter,
Antonio, Giuseppe.

Water rises,
pasta turns in the pot,
a table is laid.

Tomato, ravioli,
a plate before her.
Silence filled
with warmth enough.
