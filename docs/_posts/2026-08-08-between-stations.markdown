---
title: Between Stations
layout: post
date: 2026-08-08 10:00:00 Z
---
She keeps the small print of their days,
timetables, receipts, the hard names of medicines.
He keeps the butcher, shed and rain,
old stories polished smooth through forty patient years.
His hands still measure fish and houses;
she reads, already knowing where the laughter falls.
Outside, the tunnel preaches darkness,
while the train runs blind through rock and buried time.
Their marriage moves like an old river,
faithful to the banks each life has made of the other.
