Marble learned the art of patience,
surrendering the body, keeping only the face.
Emperor, god and nameless citizen,
centuries pared to cheekbone, mouth and broken nose.
At the line’s end, an old man pauses,
offering his living profile without plinth or label.
He studies something beyond the stone,
perhaps asking what memory must cut away.
One day, another stranger will stand here,
wondering which face time carved with greater care.
