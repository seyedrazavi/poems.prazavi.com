---
title: Load Bearing
layout: post
date: 2026-08-23 10:00:00 Z
---
A pillar bears the unread morning,
while neither traveller lifts his eyes to stone.
One leans his day into a suitcase,
already departing before his body follows.
The other claims a cool square of shade,
a chapter open, gold breath warm beside him.
Above, light writes its bright, illegible script,
across the column no one thinks to cross.
One life is counted out in departures;
one in pages, while the dog dreams beyond both.
