title: Standing Ovation
layout: post
date: 2026-08-39 10:00:00 Z
---
“Heartbreaking. Soulful. A masterpiece.”
The poster tells them what the evening ought to mean.
Five stars flare above their tired heads,
while two young bodies sag beneath tomorrow.
Her dress still glitters from another room;
his shirt is folded into journeys not yet made.
She rests against the oldest pillow,
a shoulder bearing what no critic thought to name.
The train will come, the praise be pasted over;
for now, he holds the whole weight of her dreaming.
