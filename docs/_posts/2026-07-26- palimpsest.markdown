---
title: Palimpsest
layout: post
date: 2026-07-26 10:00:00 Z
---
The window has forgotten itself,blurred by rain, fingerprints and years of breath.
Outside, platforms loosen into trees;inside, a man borrows an hour from a book.
His face fractures through passing stations,paragraphs and ghosts gathering in the glass.
The page waits while the train edits distance,each field laid over the one that came before.
Memory keeps nothing whole, only layered,the words you read beneath the life reading you.
