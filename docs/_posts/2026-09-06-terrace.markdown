date: 2026-09-06 10:00:00 Z

Two stands beside three beneath the rain,
three beside four, each certain it stands alone.
The columns know their common burden,
one stone spine shouldering the weathered row.
An old man passes, hands linked behind,
as though returning the last years of a borrowed life.
Five, six, seven, the houses count,
children into uniforms, lovers into framed ghosts.
Still the pavement asks one footstep more,
while the city counts the man who thinks he is passing.
